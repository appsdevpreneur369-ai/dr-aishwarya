Once your implant crown is fitted it looks and works like a natural tooth. It needs the same care, and in one way a little more attention, because the gum around an implant has less natural defence than the gum around a real tooth.

## The main risk: peri-implantitis

Plaque left around an implant can inflame the gum (**peri-implant mucositis**). If that continues, the bone supporting the implant can start to shrink: this is **peri-implantitis**, the most common reason implants fail years after they were placed. The good news is that it is largely preventable.

## Every day

1. **Brush twice a day** with a soft brush, paying attention to where the crown meets the gum.
2. **Clean between teeth** with interdental brushes, implant floss or a water flosser.
3. **Don't smoke.** Smoking is one of the strongest risk factors for implant problems.

## Watch for

- Bleeding when you clean around the implant
- Redness or swelling of the gum
- A bad taste, or a feeling that the crown is loose

Call the clinic if you notice any of these. Problems caught early are usually simple to treat.

## Check-ups

Regular reviews let Dr. Aishwarya check the gum and bone around the implant and clean areas you can't reach. If you grind your teeth at night, ask about a protective night guard.
