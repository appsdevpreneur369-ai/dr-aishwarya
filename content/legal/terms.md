## Online booking

- Your slot is secured once the confirmation screen appears or the confirmation message arrives on your mobile. A request sent through the enquiry form or WhatsApp is not yet a confirmed appointment; the clinic will contact you to agree a time.
- Please give accurate details. The mobile code we send is there to stop bookings in someone else's name.
- To change or cancel, use your patient account or call the clinic as early as possible.
- Appointment times are a guide. Emergencies or longer treatments can occasionally cause a short wait.

## Information on this site

Treatment descriptions are general information and not advice for your situation. See our medical disclaimer.

## Acceptable use

Do not misuse the booking system, for example by making false or repeated bookings, or try to interfere with the website's security.
