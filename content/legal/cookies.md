This website keeps browser storage to a minimum and uses **no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies**.

## What we store

- **Session storage**, holding a single flag so the appointment pop-up appears at most once per visit. It is cleared when you close the tab.

## Third parties

- **Google Maps** is loaded only when you tap to show the map on the contact or home page. At that point Google's own privacy policy applies to any cookies it places.
- **WhatsApp** links open WhatsApp, which applies its own policies.

## No profiling

We do not build advertising profiles, track you across other websites or sell any information about your visit. Booking details you type are sent only when you press the confirm button, directly to the clinic's ClinicFlow247 booking system.

## Your choices

You can clear or block site data in your browser settings at any time. The site works fully without it; the booking pop-up may simply appear once more.
