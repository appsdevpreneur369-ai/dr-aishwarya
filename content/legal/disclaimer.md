The information on this website is written to help you understand dental conditions and treatments in general terms. It is **not** a diagnosis and does not replace an examination by a qualified dentist.

- Every mouth is different. Whether a treatment suits you, how many visits it needs and what it costs can only be decided after an examination.
- Visit counts and healing times on this site are typical ranges, not promises.
- Representative photographs are labelled as such and do not show our patients or results.
- Articles in our Learn section are written for patients and are reviewed by Dr. Aishwarya before they are marked as reviewed. Medical knowledge changes, so the date on each article shows when it was last updated.
- Nothing on this website creates a dentist–patient relationship. That begins when you are examined at the clinic.
- Severe pain, a swollen face, trouble breathing or swallowing, or bleeding that won't stop all need urgent help: phone us on the emergency line, or head to a hospital casualty department without delay. An online booking is not the right route.
