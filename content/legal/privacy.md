Aishwarya Dental Clinic and Implant Centre, 4-5-57, beside Sai Baba Road, opp. EMGEE House, Vidyanagar, Guntur, Andhra Pradesh 522007 ("we", "the clinic") is responsible for the personal data described here. This policy follows India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

## Information we collect

- **When you book:** your name, mobile number, optional email address, the concern you select and your chosen time.
- **When you visit:** your medical and dental history, examination findings, X-rays, treatment plans, prescriptions and invoices.
- **When you use the website:** basic technical information needed to keep it secure, such as server logs with your IP address and browser type.

The website uses no advertising or tracking cookies.

## How we use it

- To schedule, confirm and remind you about appointments.
- To examine and treat you, and to keep your clinical records.
- With your consent, to send a thank-you after your visit and an invitation to leave honest feedback on Google. These messages never contain medical details, and you can opt out at any time.
- To meet our legal and professional obligations.

We do not sell your data or share it for marketing.

## Our software provider

Bookings, patient records and messages are handled on **ClinicFlow247**, which processes data for us under our instructions. Messages are delivered through its WhatsApp, SMS and email services. *(Storage location and processor agreement to be confirmed before publishing.)*

## How long we keep it

Clinical records are kept for the period required by law and professional guidance. Enquiries that did not turn into a visit are removed after a reasonable period.

## Your rights

You have the right to a copy of your data, to have mistakes fixed, to have it deleted where the law allows, to stop future messages, and to name someone who can use these rights on your behalf. Complaints can also be taken to the Data Protection Board of India.
